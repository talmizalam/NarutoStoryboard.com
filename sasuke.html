The Story of Sasuke Uchiha

◉ The Last Uchiha – Childhood —

Sasuke Uchiha was born in Konohagakure (Hidden Leaf Village) into the powerful Uchiha clan. The Uchiha were known for their Sharingan

a special eye technique that gave them incredible combat abilities.

p

As a child, Sasuke admired only one person: his older brother, Itachi Uchiha. Itachi was a prodigy — calm, intelligent, and incredibly strong.

Sasuke trained every day just to be acknowledged by him.

◉ How looks Sasuke in child age !

But everything changed one night.

When Sasuke was still young, he returned home to find his entire clan murdered. His parents… his relatives… everyone was dead.

And standing in front of him was Itachi.

Itachi told Sasuke he killed the clan to test his abilities. He forced Sasuke to relive the massacre through a powerful illusion (Tsukuyomi),

mentally breaking him.

Sasuke:

“If you want to kill me… hate me. Grow stronger.”

p

From that moment, Sasuke lived only for revenge.

Team 7 – Rivalry with Naruto —

How look sasuke in Genin

Sasuke joined the Ninja Academy and quickly became the top student. He was cool, talented, and distant.

p

He was placed in Team 7 with:

• Naruto Uzumaki (his loud rival)

• Sakura Haruno (who had a crush on him)

• Kakashi Hatake (their teacher)

At first, Sasuke saw Naruto as weak and annoying. But Naruto’s constant improvement started threatening his pride.

During missions like:

• The Land of Waves arc

• The Chunin Exams

• The fight against Gaara

Sasuke began realizing Naruto was catching up.

Then came another turning point.

◉ The Curse Mark & Growing Darkness —

During the Chunin Exams, Orochimaru attacked Sasuke and gave him a Curse Mark, increasing his power but slowly corrupting him.

Sasuke became obsessed with gaining strength — especially after seeing Naruto learn powerful techniques like Rasengan.

When Itachi returned to the village and easily defeated him again, Sasuke felt humiliated.

He realized:

Staying in the village would never make him strong enough.

So he made the painful decision to leave Konoha and join Orochimaru, a dangerous rogue ninja who promised him power.

Naruto tried to stop him. They fought at the Valley of the End. It was one of the most emotional battles in Naruto history.

Sasuke defeated Naruto but refused to kill him — because he didn’t want to gain power the same way Itachi did.

Then he left the village.

◉ Training Under Orochimaru —

For nearly three years, Sasuke trained under Orochimaru.

He became:

• Faster

• Smarter

• More ruthless

• Master of advanced Sharingan

Eventually, Sasuke realized Orochimaru only wanted his body.

He defeated Orochimaru and formed his own team: Team Hebi (later Taka):

• Suigetsu

• Karin

• Jugo

◉ The Battle with Itachi —

Finally, the long-awaited battle happened.

Sasuke vs Itachi.

It was intense — genjutsu, fire techniques, Susanoo, Amaterasu.

In the end, Itachi died from illness and exhaustion. But then came the biggest twist.

Tobi (Obito Uchiha) revealed the truth:

• Itachi didn’t kill the clan for power.

• The Uchiha were planning a coup.

• The village leaders ordered Itachi to massacre the clan to prevent civil war.

• Itachi spared only Sasuke… because he loved him.

Sasuke’s entire life was built on a lie.

His hatred shifted. Now he didn’t want revenge on Itachi. He wanted revenge on Konoha.

◉ Sasuke the Revolutionary —

Sasuke joined the Akatsuki and attacked the Five Kage Summit. He became darker, colder, more unstable.

His Sharingan evolved into Mangekyo Sharingan. Later he gained Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan.

During the Fourth Great Ninja War, Sasuke was revived into the battle after meeting the reanimated Itachi.

Itachi helped Sasuke understand the truth more deeply — but didn’t force him to forgive the village.

After meeting the previous Hokage (Hashirama and others), Sasuke formed his own conclusion:

He decided he would destroy the current system and become a “revolutionary Hokage” — someone who rules through fear and unites the world by

making everyone hate him.

◉ Final Battle – Naruto vs Sasuke ⚔️

After defeating Kaguya together, Sasuke revealed his plan. Naruto stood against him. They fought again at the Valley of the End.

This fight wasn’t about power.

It was about:

• Ideals

• Pain

• Loneliness

• Friendship

Naruto refused to give up on him. Both lost an arm in the final clash.

Finally…

Sasuke admitted defeat. Not just in battle — but emotionally. For the first time, he accepted Naruto as his true friend.
