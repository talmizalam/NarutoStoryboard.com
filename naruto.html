The Story of Naruto

◉ The Boy No One Wanted

The story starts in the Hidden Leaf Village (Konoha). Years ago, a terrifying monster called the Nine-Tailed Fox attacked the village.

To save everyone, the Fourth Hokage, Minato Namikaze, sealed the beast inside a newborn baby — his own son.

That baby was Naruto Uzumaki.

Naruto grew up alone. The villagers feared him because the Nine-Tails (Kurama) was sealed inside him. He didn’t understand why people hated him.

So he decided:

“I’ll become Hokage, and everyone will acknowledge me!”

◉ Naruto’s Ninja Journey Begins -

Naruto joins the Ninja Academy and struggles because he isn’t naturally talented. After many failures, he finally graduates

and is placed in Team 7, led by Kakashi Hatake.

His teammates:

• Sasuke Uchiha – A genius ninja seeking revenge.

• Sakura Haruno – Intelligent and strong-willed.

They complete dangerous missions and begin forming bonds.

◉ Sasuke’s Dark Path -

Sasuke’s entire clan was killed by his older brother, Itachi Uchiha. Sasuke lives only for revenge.

When he feels Naruto is becoming stronger than him, jealousy grows. Eventually, Sasuke leaves the village to gain more power

from the villain Orochimaru.

Naruto tries to stop him. They fight at the Valley of the End in an emotional battle. Naruto loses — and Sasuke leaves.

This becomes Naruto’s lifelong mission:

To bring Sasuke back.

◉ Naruto Shippuden – The War Begins -

Naruto Shippuden – The War Begins

A dangerous organization called Akatsuki begins capturing the tailed beasts, including the Nine-Tails inside Naruto.

Members like:

• Pain

• Madara Uchiha

• Obito Uchiha

create chaos across the ninja world.

◉ Jiraiya’s Death & Naruto’s Growth -

Naruto’s mentor Jiraiya is killed by Pain. Naruto is heartbroken but doesn’t give up. He trains harder and learns Sage Mode.

When Pain destroys the Leaf Village, Naruto returns and defeats him — but instead of revenge, Naruto chooses forgiveness.

This changes everything.

◉ The Fourth Great Ninja War -

Madara and Obito start a world war to trap everyone in an illusion called Infinite Tsukuyomi.

Naruto befriends Kurama (the Nine-Tails), unlocking massive power. He fights alongside all villages, uniting them.

Naruto and Sasuke fight together and seal Kaguya.

◉ Final Battle – Naruto vs Sasuke -

After the war, Sasuke wants to change the world through control and revolution.

Naruto refuses to give up on him.

They fight one last time at the Valley of the End. Both lose an arm. Exhausted, Sasuke finally accepts Naruto’s friendship.

He returns to the village.

◉ Naruto Becomes Hokage -

Years later, Naruto achieves his dream.

He becomes the Seventh Hokage of the Hidden Leaf Village. The lonely boy who was hated by everyone becomes

the hero who saved the world

He marries Hinata Hyuga, and they have children — leading into the next series, Boruto: Naruto Next Generations.
